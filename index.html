Research paper in submission

Amazon Bedrock Guardrails · Deep Engine-Science · Seattle, WA

I developed Judge2Vec, an unsupervised framework that selects compact, complementary LLM juries without human labels for selection. It estimates criterion-specific competence embeddings from leave-one-family-out agreement with a family-balanced consensus on unlabeled pilot examples.

Using 4 of 19 judges, Judge2Vec ranked first among baselines on 8 text benchmarks. Average Kendall correlation was 0.467 versus 0.424 for full-pool aggregation, with 83.9% lower average deployment cost ($4.67 versus $28.97 per 1,000 items).

I also developed budget-adaptive jury replacement and zero-shot cross-task transfer. Juries constructed without target-task examples retained 98.5% performance. Evaluation also included multimodal and agentic settings.

AT&T Chief Data Office

Graduate Research Intern

Research paper in submission

Bedminster, NJ · Mentors: Divesh Srivastava and Yaron Kanza

I developed a kNN-LM framework for private-data question answering and selective forgetting without retraining the LLM or embedding function. The embedding design preserves accurate next-token retrieval while protecting against leakage of deleted private associations. Sensitive values are masked during embedding training; correct next tokens remain retrievable from the private database until deletion.

The framework achieved 73.8% answer accuracy versus 60.0% for RAG.

I implemented FAISS retrieval and user-level logical deletion without index rebuilds. Scaling the synthetic datastore 1,000 times to 19.6 million key–value pairs increased answer-generation time 4.9 times, from 1.42 to 7.01 seconds per query.

Selected Research Projects

Person distillation

Preprint · 2026

I formulated person distillation as transforming heterogeneous personal traces into faithful, evidence-grounded, and revisable representations of an individual's knowledge, expertise, judgment, communication style, behavior, values, personality, and relationships. The work establishes an end-to-end lifecycle covering source evidence, person representations, distillation methods, and evaluation, and organizes existing approaches into six method families. It also proposes an evaluation framework for assessing person-specific fidelity, evidence grounding, consistency over time, and the ability to recognize claims unsupported by available evidence.

PaperWebsiteGitHub

LLM-based agent generalizability

ACM Computing Surveys · 2026

I defined agent generalizability across instructions, tasks, environments, and domains, and organized evaluation and improvement methods by the backbone LLM, specialized components, and their interactions. The survey distinguishes frameworks that can be adapted to different settings from agents that generalize beyond their fine-tuning data.

MPCEval: Multi-party conversation evaluation

KDD 2026 · Oral presentation

I developed a reference-free, reproducible benchmark for multi-party conversation generation, with turn- and conversation-level metrics for speaker modeling, content quality, and speaker–content consistency. We benchmarked comprehensive methods across datasets spanning collaborative problem-solving, scripted dialogue, and real-world enterprise discussions.

ACM SIGKDD 2026, Datasets and Benchmarks Track

Privacy attacks and defenses

I studied membership-inference attacks on teacher–student pairs and proposed mitigations for privacy leakage in knowledge distillation. I also developed a temporal attack on sequential data releases using hidden Markov models and reinforcement learning. I also studied privacy attacks targeting data buyers in data markets and developed defenses to protect buyer privacy.
